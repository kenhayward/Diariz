Rather than a strip of tabs, opening a meeting shows you what it contains.

## The summary card

At the top is the meeting's **summary shown inline**, with no hover and no extra click, plus:

- The **meeting type** dropdown, which decides the minutes template and which formulas are offered.
- Key facts as chips: date and time, duration, whether the audio is still available and how long it
  has left, language, the speakers, who recorded it, and which rooms it is in.

## The tiles

Below the card is a grid of tiles for **Transcript, Actions, Speakers, Notes, Files, and Formulas**.
Each shows its real count and a preview of what is inside, so you can see what a meeting holds without
opening anything.

Notes, Files, and Formulas can be added or run straight from their tile. Clicking any tile drills into
that section with a breadcrumb back to the hub, and Diariz remembers which section you were last in.

## The meeting card

If the recording is linked to a calendar meeting, a card below the tiles shows its time, location,
organiser, and description, with **Change** and **Unlink** actions. If it is not linked yet but your
calendar suggests a match, the suggestion appears ready to accept in one click. The card is hidden
when there is nothing to show.

## Naming

A meeting gets an automatic title, and the summariser fills in a proper name once it runs. You can
rename it yourself at any time, and your name always wins.
