Diariz turns spoken conversations into something you can read, search, and reason about.

## The basic loop

1. **Capture.** Record from your microphone, from system audio (so you get both sides of a call), or
   upload an audio file you already have.
2. **Transcribe.** The audio is sent to a transcription worker that produces a timestamped transcript
   with each speaker separated out. This happens in the background, so you can close the page.
3. **Understand.** Once the transcript exists, Diariz can summarise it, draft meeting minutes, pull out
   action items, and tag it with the topics it was actually about.
4. **Ask.** Chat over one meeting, a folder of meetings, or your whole library.

## Where things live

- The **left panel** lists your meetings. It also has tabs for Actions and Tags, and a calendar view.
- Opening a meeting lands you on its **hub**: a summary card plus tiles for Transcript, Actions,
  Speakers, Notes, Files, and Formulas.
- The **account menu** is the small avatar at the top left, above the meetings list. It holds Preferences,
  Settings, the guided tour, and this help.

## What you need

Transcription runs on the server, so nothing needs installing to record from a browser. The features
that involve an AI model, such as summaries, minutes, action items, tags, chat, and formulas, need an
LLM endpoint configured either by an administrator or by you. See [AI and model settings](/help/ai-model-settings).

The desktop app adds system-audio capture, tray recording, and meeting screenshots.
